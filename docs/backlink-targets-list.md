## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Personalize every outreach** - Reference specific article/content
2. **Lead with value** - Show why YOUR resource helps THEIR audience
3. **Follow-up once** - After 2 weeks if no response
4. **Track carefully** - Use spreadsheet to avoid duplicate outreach
5. **Build relationships** - First link often leads to second opportunity
6. **Timing matters** - Avoid holidays, send Tue-Thu 10am-2pm
7. **Keep it short** - 2-3 paragraph emails get highest response
8. **Test subject lines** - A/B test to improve open rate

## 🚫 Sites to Avoid

- ❌ Low-DA directories (< 30 DA)
- ❌ Non-English sites (unless targeting)
- ❌ Unrelated niches (e.g., "How to Cook Bacon")
- ❌ Spammy/PBN-looking sites
- ❌ Sites with high outbound link density (>50 links/page)
- ❌ Pages with obvious anchor text manipulation
- ❌ Keyword-stuffed resource pages
