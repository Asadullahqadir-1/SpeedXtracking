# Backlink Outreach Strategy - Speed X Tracking

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the manual backlink acquisition strategy for speedxtracking.org to improve domain authority and SERP rankings. With a single-carrier focus being perceived as "thin content" by AdSense reviewers, this strategy focuses on topical authority through editorial outreach.

**Target:** 15-20 high-quality backlinks from relevant domains in Year 1

---

### Angle 1: Free Parcel Tracking Tools (Authority Build)
**Theme:** "Best Free Shipping Tracking Tools Comparison"

**Target:** Tech blogs, productivity sites, shipping education sites

## Outreach Templates

### Template 1: General Resource Request

**Subject Line:** *"Valuable resource for [site topic] - [specific angle]"*

```
Hi [Editor/Owner Name],

I noticed your recent article on [specific article topic]. Your readers dealing with [shipping/tracking issue] might benefit from our comprehensive guide: [Your URL].

We've created a free resource that covers:
- [Key benefit 1]
- [Key benefit 2]
- [Key benefit 3]

Would this be a good fit to mention in your article or a future roundup?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Speed X Tracking
```

**Success Rate:** ~20-25%

---

### Template 2: Guest Post Pitch

**Subject Line:** *"Guest post: [Topic] for [Publication]"*

```
Hi [Editor Name],

I'd like to contribute a guest post to [Publication] on the topic: "[Specific angle + keyword]"

Our proposal:
- **Title:** "[Specific, keyword-rich title]"
- **Angle:** [Clear problem/solution]
- **Key Points:** [3-5 bullet points of unique insight]
- **Word Count:** [Target: 1500-2500 words]

This would be valuable for your audience because [specific reason related to their content].

Would you be interested? I can provide writing samples if helpful.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Speed X Tracking
```

**Success Rate:** ~15-20%

**Topics to pitch:**
- "7 Shipping Problems Every E-commerce Seller Faces (And How to Fix Them)"
- "The Complete Guide to Customs Clearance Delays"
- "Tracking API Integration: A Developer's Guide"
- "Why Your Package Says 'Out for Delivery' But Doesn't Arrive (And What To Do)"

---

### Template 3: Roundup Contribution

**Subject Line:** *"New resource for your '[topic]' roundup"*

```
Hi [Editor Name],

I saw your roundup of "[topic/tools/resources]". We've recently created [specific resource type] that might fit well:

**[Resource Title]**
[Brief description - 1-2 sentences]
- Best for: [specific use case]
- Cost: Free
- Key feature: [standout feature]

Link: [Your URL]

If this aligns with what you're looking for, I'd be happy to provide more details or adjust the description to match your format.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Speed X Tracking
```

**Success Rate:** ~30-40% (highest success rate)

---

### Template 4: Broken Link Building

**Subject Line:** *"Potential resource for your [topic] page"*

```
Hi [Site Owner],

I was reading your article on [article title] and noticed you reference [broken/outdated resource]. 

We've published a comprehensive guide on the same topic that might be more current: [Your URL]

Would you consider updating the link to our resource?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
Speed X Tracking
```

**Success Rate:** ~15%

**Finding opportunities:**
- Use Ahrefs, SEMrush, or Moz to find broken links on relevant sites
- Look for outdated tracking guides
- Find missing resources in relevant roundups

## Monthly Outreach Cadence

### Month 1-2: Foundation Building
- Identify and compile 30 target sites
- Create 2-3 core content pieces (guest post drafts)
- Personalize outreach templates
- Start with warm outreach (sites you already have audience on)

### Month 3-6: Active Outreach
- Send 3-5 personalized outreach emails per week
- Follow up on non-responses after 2 weeks
- Publish guest posts (2-3 minimum)
- Share roundup contributions

### Month 7-12: Diversification
- Expand to developer/API angle
- Launch free tool/comparison resource (attracts backlinks)
- Create infographic (highly linkable asset)
- Reach out for featured mentions

## Tracking & Monitoring

### What to Track
1. **Outreach volume** - Emails sent, responses received, conversion %
2. **Backlink acquisition** - New links acquired per month
3. **Link quality** - DA (Domain Authority), spam score, topical relevance
4. **Rankings** - Track keyword movements for target keywords
5. **Referral traffic** - Traffic generated from backlinks

### Tools for Monitoring
- **Google Search Console** - Monitor new backlinks, click-through rate
- **Ahrefs** - Link quality, competitor analysis, rank tracking
- **SEMrush** - Backlink monitoring, rank tracking
- **Moz Link Explorer** - DA tracking

### Monthly Reporting
Track in a spreadsheet:
| Date | Target Site | Contact | Angle | Status | Backlink Acquired | Notes |

## Success Metrics

**Year 1 Goals:**
- ✅ 15-20 quality backlinks acquired
- ✅ 3-5 guest posts published
- ✅ Domain Authority increase of 5-10 points
- ✅ 20-30% improvement in target keyword rankings
- ✅ 30-50% increase in referral traffic from backlinks

**Quality Over Quantity:**
- Only pursue links from relevant, high-quality sites (DA 20+)
- Avoid PBNs, low-quality directories, and guest post networks
- Focus on topical relevance (shipping/logistics > general tech)

---

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Avoid:**
- Paid link brokers
- Low-DA bulk guest posting networks
- Reciprocal linking schemes
- Private blog networks (PBNs)
- Directory submissions (except DMOZ if still open)
- Low-quality ".tk" / ".ml" domains
- Non-contextual link placement

✅ **Pursue:**
- Contextual editorial links
- Natural guest post opportunities
- Industry expert mentions
- Award/recognition links
- Genuine resource recommendations
